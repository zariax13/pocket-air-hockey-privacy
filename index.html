Privacy Policy for Pocket Air Hockey

Effective Date: October 2026

Summary: Pocket Air Hockey does NOT collect, store, share, or transmit any user data or personal information. The game works 100% offline with zero advertisements and zero tracking SDKs.

1. Information Collection and Use

Pocket Air Hockey ("we", "our", or "the Game") is built with user privacy as a core principle. We do not collect any personal data, identifiers, or usage statistics.

  • No Account Needed: No registration, login, or personal profile is required.
  • No Analytics: We do not include any tracking or telemetry libraries.
  • No Advertisements: No ad networks or targeted marketing code are present.
  • No Internet Permission: The game does not request android.permission.INTERNET. All gameplay is purely local.

2. Permissions Used

The Game only requests the following permission:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used strictly to emit vibration/haptic feedback during paddle collisions, wall bounces, and goal celebrations.

3. Children's Privacy

The Game is family-friendly and compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K. We do not collect personal information from children of any age.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the developer support email provided on our Google Play Store page.
